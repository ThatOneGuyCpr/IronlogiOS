# Exercise clips

Drop a clip in this folder named after the exercise ID, for example `db_bench.mp4`. The app plays it in place of the online video.

- Format: .mp4 or .mov (H.264 or HEVC), ideally 720p, 5 to 15 seconds, no audio needed. It loops.
- Keep each file under about 10 MB. GitHub's web uploader caps files at 25 MB.
